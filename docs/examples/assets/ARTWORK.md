# Artwork and diagrams

The six scene illustrations were generated with the built-in imagegen tool in text-to-image mode. They are conceptual illustrations, not screenshots of Jul.IA or tested application integrations. The WebP files are optimized exports of those illustrations; the original generated PNGs were preserved.

The two topology diagrams and their phone layouts are editable SVG. Their labels describe responsibilities and a possible deployment; they do not assert an automatic sharing or identity workflow.
