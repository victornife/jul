# Jul.IA — Build Anything. Share It on Your Terms.

## Our vision

Make software easier to publish, protect and share, while keeping control over where it runs, how it is shared and who can access it.

## The problem

AI is making software creation faster and more personal. More people can build useful tools for their own needs, teams and communities.

Sharing them can still become a second infrastructure project. Making an application reachable, controlling access and keeping it running can demand more time, expertise and expense than expected. Even a small application may need advanced protection and traffic controls.

**As software becomes easier to build, sharing it on your own terms matters more.**

## Our mission

Give people and teams the freedom to run and share their own software, with advanced capabilities and control over their infrastructure and dependencies, while reducing unnecessary cost and complexity.

## Our three principles

**1. Power without unnecessary complexity**

Keep advanced capabilities within reach, even for small applications. Match infrastructure and operating effort to actual needs.

**2. Autonomy by design**

Run software where you choose. Use external platforms where they help, understand the dependencies and retain the freedom to change direction.

**3. Sharing with chosen groups**

Make applications easier to share with family, collaborators, communities and teams. Keep public publishing an equally valid choice.

## From gateway to sharing

Today, our portable, open-source application gateway brings routing, protection, traffic controls and an embedded Console together. You can run it in your homelab, on your own server or in the cloud, alongside tools such as Cloudflare Tunnel and Tailscale.

We want to build a simpler sharing experience around that foundation, from establishing connectivity to choosing audiences and managing access. We have not yet brought those steps into one integrated experience.

**The gateway is our foundation. Sharing is our destination.**

## Freedom to grow

Start small, explore advanced capabilities and grow as your needs develop. Jul.IA can be a useful middle step, and some applications may stay with us indefinitely. When an application outgrows our model, we want to help make the next step understandable and keep the path to other infrastructure open.

## Our commitment

Our standalone core is free and open source, without a mandatory Jul-hosted service. Hosting, connectivity and operating time still have costs. Secure operation depends on the application and its deployment.

**Build it. Run it where you choose. Share it on your terms.**
