## Continuously exercised contracts

The real-package harness is [scripts/ga-soak.py](../../scripts/ga-soak.py).
Steady probes repeat after a 200 ms pause; an independent traffic worker continues
routing and allowed/denied auth requests during the fault and reload phases.
The serial negative/recovery cycle repeats every 60 seconds. No required phase
may disappear for more than 90 seconds, including initial and final coverage.

| Contract | Required observations; any counterexample fails |
| --- | --- |
| Routing | Exact predicate route requires GET, X-Tenant=public and version=v2; method/header/query mismatches fall through to regex. Long non-root prefix beats regex; root fallback is separate. Ordered set/add yields two header values, removal suppresses backend X-Powered-By, and the generation marker is read back on publish/rollback. |
| CORS | Allowed and denied origins; approved POST/X-Tenant preflight terminates at 204; unapproved preflight follows normal routing without an allow-origin grant. A denied preflight is **not** assumed to produce 403. |
| HTTP admission | Four concurrent one-second requests against active=1/pending=1/socket=1: observe one active and one pending, exactly one 200 and three attributed 503s. Retain admission counters and state. |
| Retry | Two distinct reset backends, two total attempts, 500 ms deadline, 5–10 ms jittered backoff and 10% retry budget; prime the budget, observe actual retries and exhaustion. Separate slow backend verifies a Jul-owned 150 ms deadline produces 504 within 100–800 ms. |
| Replay boundaries | Separate **unbudgeted**, two-attempt pool: unsafe POST reaches exactly one backend; a committed truncated response reaches exactly one backend and returns an incomplete, unchanged nonempty prefix, never a replacement response. Aborted net/http writes need not flush their final buffered bytes. |
| Circuit and health | Passive transport failure opens the one-second circuit, then a request recovers it; a separate active HTTP health signal excludes and readmits a backend. State and successful recovery are mandatory, not merely injected errors. |
| Cancellation | Close a slow HTTP client and reset held native gRPC streams; active and pending slots must return to zero. Cancellation is not a backend outage. |
| Native gRPC | Valid h2c unary echo with verified protobuf bytes; hold one active and one queued stream, require overload HTTP 503, then cancel both and verify release. As documented, grpc-go maps this HTTP response to UNAVAILABLE; the server is not required to send a grpc-status trailer on this rejection. |
| L4 | A single TCP connection echoes through every policy generation; bounded short-connection churn, an over-limit close and injected backend close followed by successful recovery. No midstream replay. |
| Auxiliary auth | Allowed IPv6 forward-auth plus explicit auth rejection; denied IPv4 destination must receive **zero** bytes. System localhost resolution is allowed initially and denied after CIDR tightening; record actual resolver addresses rather than inventing an authoritative-DNS fixture. |
| Discovery and redirects | Consul-protocol auxiliary polling, last-good backend retention on an un-followed redirect, and generation-policy tightening: retired workers/connections must not fetch or publish the deliberately changed backend. Forward-auth redirects are relayed, not followed. The prohibited redirect destination must never be contacted. |
| Publication and rollback | Tighten egress and change routing policy under continuing traffic; prove new policy immediately, reject a malformed candidate without advancing base_version, rollback through recorded history, and verify original route/auth behavior and the original long-lived TCP connection. |

This selects bounded native gRPC/TCP paths. It does **not** certify UDP,
transcoding, active-stream replay, JWKS redirect following, mixed authoritative
DNS answers, trusted-name rebinding, backend-egress enforcement, full Consul
deployment, #406 outcome-aware retry, #522 ejection or unrelated Beta features.
Existing focused contract tests supplement, but do not replace, the real-package
workload.

## Fixed acceptance thresholds and retained evidence

- **Duration:** at least 28,800 measured seconds; at least 2,881 successful
  samples, taken every ten seconds, with no interval over 25 seconds.
- **Continuity:** one surviving Jul process; wall-clock versus monotonic and
  boot-time discontinuities over five seconds fail. WSL suspension invalidates
  the attempt; do not add sleeping time or combine recording windows.
- **Failures:** zero unexpected probe failures. Expected statuses are bound to
  the specific injected phase, not a generic allowed 5xx percentage.
  All required phases must recur within 90 seconds.
- **RSS:** maximum 1 GiB, post-ten-minute-warmup peak growth at most 128 MiB,
  and regression slope over the final four active hours at most 16 MiB/hour.
- **Heap and goroutines:** heap-in-use peak growth at most 128 MiB and
  goroutine peak growth at most 96 above the warmup maxima.
- **Cardinality:** retained non-histogram-bucket metric-series union at most
  warmup union +128 and never over 3,000.
- **Quiescence:** stop and join all clients, close the long TCP connection,
  wait 120 seconds, and assess all three samples in the final 20-second
  window. Active and pending admission must be zero; FDs at most warmup
  maximum +8; goroutines at most warmup maximum +96.
- **Profiles and space:** nonempty heap/goroutine profiles before and after,
  timed metrics and fixture counts, phase/outcome records, configuration and
  policy identities, result/failure and complete artifact digests. Evidence
  reaching 2 GiB fails; access logs rotate at 16 MiB with four backups.
  The retained rotated window is **not** a complete request archive.
- **Cleanup:** terminate only the Jul/backend processes created by the run;
  close fixture listeners. Retain failed attempts and private evidence rather
  than deleting or overwriting them. An existing output directory is refused.

Short preflight runs cannot satisfy criterion 5. They exercise the same phase
assertions and negative acceptance tests, but do not assess the noisy
sub-hour RSS slope or imply eight hours of stability.

## Reproduction and evidence handling

Follow the [operator procedure](../soak-procedures.md#issue-590-eight-hour-real-package-candidate).
Run deterministic acceptance tests and a 120-second real-package preflight
before the long service. Pin native package architecture and the existing
gRPC fixture separately; do not rebuild Jul.

Each directory contains `manifest.json`, exact harness copies, `events.jsonl`,
`samples.jsonl`, live progress, profile metadata, `result.json` or `failure.json`,
and `digests.json`. `private-runtime/` contains configuration/history, raw
access records and profiles. Keep the directory owner-only. Do not publish
raw configuration, credentials, bodies or private profiles in GitHub.
Publish bounded synthetic counts, sanitised metrics/outcomes and digests with
the source-bound ledger instead.

## Decision and closure boundary

The running process writes evidence; it never edits maturity or closes issues.
After completion, check the service exit/OOM state, final digests, complete
duration/phase/resource/cleanup evidence and every explicit limitation.
Only a passing, reviewed eight-hour `criterion_5_candidate` may justify
changing **exactly** SEC-EGRESS, CGC-ROUTE and CGC-RES in
[feature-status.yaml](../feature-status.yaml) to GA/soaked with criterion 5=true.

Then update status, feature guides, the dated GA review's successor note,
soak evidence, relevant release/compatibility notices and changelog; regenerate
owned mirrors and run documentation/generated checks. Record commands/results,
reviewed/merged identities and residuals in #590 and #62; update #579's
separate stability-lane entry state. Close #590 only after its acceptance
checklist is evidenced. An interrupted, missing or failed result leaves it open.
