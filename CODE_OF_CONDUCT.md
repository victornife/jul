# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We, as members, contributors, and maintainers of Jul.IA, pledge to make
participation in the project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Showing empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes, apologizing where appropriate, and
  learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.

Jul.IA is a technical project. Good-faith debate, strong disagreement, and
criticism of code, design choices, project decisions, or direction are welcome.
Keep criticism focused on the work and avoid personal attacks.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, personal attacks, or discriminatory
  language.
- Harassment, threats, or repeated unwanted contact.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their explicit
  permission.
- Retaliating against someone for raising a concern or participating in an
  investigation.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in Jul.IA project spaces, including the GitHub
repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and reviews. It also applies to
direct messages and other communications when they concern Jul.IA or arise from
participation in the project.

It applies when someone is officially representing Jul.IA in public, including
when acting or speaking as an appointed project representative.

Maintainers may take action in spaces the project controls in response to
conduct covered by this Code of Conduct.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to
**[vniharrafe@gmail.com](mailto:vniharrafe@gmail.com)**. Please include links,
dates, relevant context, and any other information that may help us understand
what happened. Do not report conduct concerns in a public issue.

If a report concerns the maintainer who receives it, that maintainer must
recuse themselves from investigating and deciding it. An uninvolved maintainer
or independent person will review the report. If no independent reviewer can be
secured, no final conduct finding or lasting sanction will be made; the reporter
will be told, and maintainers may take temporary, proportionate steps in
project-controlled spaces to protect people while the matter remains unresolved.

Maintainers will review reports promptly and fairly, limit access to the report
to people who need it to respond, and avoid sharing identifying details where
possible. We cannot promise absolute confidentiality: information may need to
be shared to investigate or respond to a report.

Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith, or helps
review one, is itself a conduct violation.

For security vulnerabilities, follow the separate instructions in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct.
They will take appropriate and fair action in response to behavior they deem
inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and
other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct. Where
appropriate, they will explain moderation decisions.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers will consider the context, severity, and impact of the behavior
when choosing a response. Responses may include:

### 1. Correction

**Community impact:** A minor incident, such as inappropriate language or
behavior that is unprofessional or unwelcome.

**Consequence:** A private, written warning explaining the concern and why the
behavior was inappropriate.

### 2. Warning

**Community impact:** A violation through a single incident or a pattern of
behavior.

**Consequence:** A warning with clear expectations and consequences for further
violations. This may include restrictions on interaction with affected people
in project-controlled spaces for a specified period.

### 3. Temporary restriction

**Community impact:** A serious violation or sustained inappropriate behavior.

**Consequence:** A temporary restriction from participation in project-controlled
spaces for a specified period. Violating the restriction may result in a
permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent ban

**Community impact:** A pattern of violations, harassment of an individual,
threats, or aggression toward or disparagement of classes of people.

**Consequence:** A permanent ban from participation in project-controlled
spaces.
